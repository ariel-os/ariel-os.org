We are co-organizing the Embedded & Resource-Constrained OS devroom at FOSDEM 2027, a dedicated space for developers, maintainers, researchers, and users of these operating systems Ariel OS, Zephyr, RIOT, Apache NuttX, Tock, Embassy, RTEMS, ThreadX, and many more to discuss how we build, test, secure, and maintain them, and how we can share more across projects.

We welcome talk proposals until Monday, November 23, 2026, at 23:59 UTC. See details below. Submit your proposal via pretalx at fosdem.org/submit .

- Devroom Date: Sunday, January 31, 2027,

  - Université libre de Bruxelles, Campus du Solbosch.

## Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

- Kernel design, scheduling, and power management
- Sharing software components across operating systems: drivers, network stacks, settings storage, and more
- Cross-compiling, simulation, and CI with real hardware in the loop: testing drivers, timing-sensitive code, board farms, and more
- Living with vendor SDKs: stale compilers, diverging forks of mbedTLS, wpa_supplicant, MicroPython, and other projects, and getting changes accepted upstream
- Designing and maintaining open reference hardware
- Bootloaders, secure and measured boot, trusted execution environments (TEEs), and keeping devices open to repurposing
- Safety and security
- Community governance
- Cool real-world projects and applications

Talks, technical deep dives, demos, and work in progress are all welcome. Talks about a single project are welcome, as long as they bring lessons, tools, or open questions that other communities can benefit from.

The morning will tentatively end with a roundtable on how our communities can work together.

## Session Formats

We expect to schedule around 5 talks and 4 lightning talks, followed by the roundtable.

    Talk: Up to 25 minutes, including Q&A
    Lightning talk: 10 minutes, great for a quick demo, a work-in-progress update, or perhaps your first FOSDEM talk

The pretalx form only offers 25 or 50 minutes. Select 25 minutes for both formats. For a lightning talk, write “Lightning talk (10 min)” at the top of the Submission Notes. Please do not select 50 minutes.

We may shorten some talks to fit the schedule and will tell you when your talk is accepted. First-time speakers are very welcome; reach out if you would like help shaping a proposal!

## Proposal Submission

Submit your proposal via pretalx at fosdem.org/submit. Create a new account if needed. Please provide the following:

- Track: Select “Embedded & Resource-Constrained OS”.
- Title and synopsis: Include a title and a short synopsis of a couple of paragraphs, with links to the projects you mention.
- Duration: Select 25 minutes. For a lightning talk, add “Lightning talk (10 min)” at the top of the Submission Notes.
- Speaker details: Include speaker name(s), affiliation(s), a short bio, and a photo in your pretalx profile.
- Co-speakers: Add them in the form. Each co-speaker needs their own pretalx account.
- License: Specify the main license(s) of the project(s) your talk is about.

Talks are given in person in Brussels. All talks are live-streamed and recorded, and the recordings are published under CC-BY.
